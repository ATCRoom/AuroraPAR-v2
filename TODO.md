- **Earth model — decided 10 Oct: no redesign on the ellipsoid.** Checked with aircraft placed exactly on the WGS84 extended centreline (LIBN, N-S and E-W runways at 45°N, 65°N, equator): with the heading *for AuroraPAR* from the CRSCalculator the lateral error of the spherical formulas is **under 1 m even at 40 NM**; with a chart (ellipsoid) heading it is 30–125 m at 10–20 NM, i.e. the problem is only the heading, already solved by the CRSCalculator. Only the **distance** suffers from the fixed radius 6371 km: 0.1–0.5 % (3–60 m at 10 NM, up to ~120 m at 20 NM; ≈ 9 ft of expected height at 10 NM), below the uncertainty of the IVAO data. **Built 10 Oct (separate build, to be tested): local Earth radius** (class `Earth` in Data.cs, Euler radius of the WGS84 ellipsoid at the threshold in the runway direction) in Distance/LateralOffset, and test traffic placed on the same sphere (exact distance on the centreline).
