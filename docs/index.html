A fork of AuroraPAR by bornac1: a Precision Approach Radar display for the Aurora ATC client, with elevation and azimuth views, a console-style analog mode, a coordination light panel and test traffic to practise with. Tracks that glide smoothly across the scope, and highly customizable: make it look like the radar you know, or start from one of nine ready-made profiles.

Version 2.0 beta, for Windows 64-bit. Free, open source (MIT). A third-party add-on for IVAO's Aurora software, not made, endorsed or supported by IVAO. For flight simulation only, never for real navigation.

Highly customizable

One program, many radars

Colours, lines, symbols, labels, units, scales, antenna position, console keys: almost everything on the screen is yours to set, and saved in a profile you can share. Nine profiles come with the program, from a plain modern display to old analog scopes. Press P to switch between them.

The profiles reproduce the look of each radar from photos and screenshots, as far as AuroraPAR can draw it. Not certified copies of real equipment.

What it does

Built for the approach

Everything a PAR controller looks at, on one screen, with the settings to make it look and behave like the radar you know.

Tracks that glide

Track drawing rebuilt from scratch: the symbol moves on every frame with the speed, course and descent of the aircraft and is pulled gently towards each new position from Aurora. No more jumps every update: the tracks flow across the scope with an incredibly smooth feel, while the history dots stay on the real positions.

Two views, like the real thing

Elevation and azimuth, with glide path, decision height, scan limits, range marks and the antenna beam that follows the tilt.

An analog console mode

Phosphor echoes lit by the sweeping beam, afterglow, knobs and backlit keys, mechanical drum readouts and optional rain clutter taken from the METAR.

Coordination light panel

The radar and tower light panel with the buzzer of the real one, on the PC and on a phone or tablet, paired by callsign.

Test traffic to practise

Fly your own aircraft on the scope: intercept from the side, change heading and rate of descent, add wind, use a keyboard, a virtual stick or a real joystick.

Yours to customise

Every colour, line style and width, custom track symbols, label layouts, NM or km, feet or metres, linear, log or angular scales, ranges up to 40 NM. Export a profile and share it.

Terrain, clutter, crosshair

Minimum altitudes or terrain under the approach from a simple text file, ground clutter near the antenna with an MTI switch, and an ILS-style deviation crosshair for the selected aircraft.

Runways without the pain

CRSCalculator works out heading, length and magnetic variation from two thresholds and checks your whole runways file. Full manual included.

Get started

Up and running in minutes

  1. Download

    Get the zip from the Releases page, check the SHA-256 and unzip it anywhere. Nothing to install.

  2. Open Aurora

    Start Aurora, allow 3rd party software access and set the traffic refresh to 0.5 s. AuroraPAR reads the traffic and the airport from it.

  3. Pick the runway

    Choose the airport and runway. Missing? Add it in the runway editor; CRSCalculator gives the exact heading.

  4. Control

    Pick a profile with P, try it with test traffic (key T), then use it on the network.

FAQ

The quick answers. The user manual has all the details.

Is it free? Is it official IVAO software?

Free and open source (MIT license). It is a third-party add-on for IVAO's Aurora, made by IVAO members: not made, endorsed or supported by IVAO. For simulation only. It is a fork of AuroraPAR by bornac1.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 64-bit and Aurora on the same PC. Nothing to install: unzip the download into a folder and run AuroraPAR.exe. Without Aurora you can still try it with test traffic.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC".

The program is not signed with a paid certificate, so SmartScreen warns the first time. Click More info → Run anyway. To be sure the file is the original one, compare its SHA-256 with SHA256SUMS.txt on the download page.

It shows STS FAIL and no traffic.

Aurora accepts programs like AuroraPAR only with Aurora → Settings → Other → Software → 3rd Party software access on. Start Aurora and AuroraPAR in any order: the connection is retried by itself. Manual 1.2

The tracks move in jumps, or DATA is red.

Aurora updates the traffic every 3 seconds by default, too slow for a PAR. In Aurora set the traffic refresh rate to 0.5 s. AuroraPAR measures it and tells you when it is too slow. Manual 3.4

My airport or runway is missing.

Add it in Settings → Runways (runways.par)...: thresholds, glide path, DH. CRSCalculator gives the exact runway heading from the two thresholds. Manual 11

An aircraft is not shown on the scope.

It is outside the scan or the antenna beam, or beyond the range. Increase the range, tilt the antenna (profiles with the narrow beam) or check the scan limits in Settings → Radar. Manual 5.1

How do I change the look of the radar?

Press P to go through the bundled profiles. To make your own, Duplicate one in Settings and change colours, lines, labels, units and scales: every change is saved in that profile. Manual 10

I lost my changes to a profile.

Reload bundled... overwrites the profiles with the same name as the bundled ones, and the changes cannot be recovered. Always work on a Duplicate (or keep an Export): your own profiles are never touched. Manual 10.1

How do I update? Are my settings kept?

AuroraPAR tells you when a new version is published and opens the download page; replace the files in your folder. Settings and profiles are kept, and the bundled profiles are updated without losing your changes. Keep a copy of runways.par and terrain.par if you edited them. Manual 1.1

Does the tower need AuroraPAR for the coordination panel?

No. The tower can use AuroraCoord (a small program with only the panel, same download page) or the panel in the browser of a phone or tablet. The two panels pair by airport and callsign. Manual 8

The coordination panel stays on "connecting..." or "waiting for the tower".

"Connecting": the internet relay is blocked, most often by a DNS ad blocker, an antivirus web shield or a VPN. "Waiting": check that both panels have the same airport, one radar and one tower, and the same panel code (or none). Manual 12

Can I practise without real traffic?

Yes: press T for test traffic. Add aircraft on final or on an intercept, give headings and rates as a controller, or fly them with a stick or a real joystick, with wind. Manual 7

Can you make the profile of another radar?

Send a Profile request with the form below, with screenshots, photos or documentation of the real display. The more detailed, the more faithful the profile.
